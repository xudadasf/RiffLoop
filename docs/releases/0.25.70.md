# 0.25.70 (114) — per-track GP guitar timbres

GP > Tracks > Track sound now offers original score sound plus nylon, steel, jazz, clean, muted, overdrive and distortion guitar sounds. Selections are saved per score and track without changing the imported GP file. Changing a timbre pauses playback; resume manually after readiness returns. The existing speed, loop, mixer and position settings are restored after MIDI reload. Embedded backing recordings retain their original sound.

Percussion and tracks sharing MIDI channels do not offer independent timbre changes, preventing a selection from changing another track. This release includes the previously verified 0.25.69 pause/stop fixes. USB reconnection continues to require manual play.

## Validation and delivery

Local tests use the bundled alphaTab synthesizer and soundfont to verify audible sample differences, unchanged notes/timing/metronome, original sound restoration and shared-channel protection. Native tests cover profile compatibility, validation and pause state; actual WKWebView integration covers switching during playback, restoring loop/speed/volume/position, resuming, reopening, restoring original sound and unchanged GP bytes. Full CI and package verification are pending. This release has not been installed on the user's iPad.

## Manual update

Download the verified Actions artifact linked in the build record and extract its IPA. Connect the unlocked iPad by USB. In Sideloadly use the existing Apple account, Remote Anisette and Apple ID Sideload, then select the new IPA and click Start. Cover-install without uninstalling the old app or changing its bundle identifier. No new authentication-component download is required for the previously working Remote setup.

After installation confirm 0.25.70 (114), existing files/history and playback controls. In GP > Tracks choose a guitar timbre, then manually resume playback. Reopen the score to check the saved selection and use Original score sound to revert. Delete the downloaded ZIP/IPA after verification if no local copies are wanted. Actual iPad/Spark MINI listening remains user verification; synthetic sound does not reproduce Guitar Pro RSE or external guitar amplifier processing.
