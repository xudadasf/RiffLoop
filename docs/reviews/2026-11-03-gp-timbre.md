## Behavior

A per-track picker offers original score sound and seven bundled guitar programs (24–30). It persists in the per-score practice profile and leaves GP bytes unchanged. Changing sound pauses playback and regenerates only the main score MIDI. The existing player-ready path restores speed, mixer, loop and position; playback resumes manually. Embedded backing audio retains its recording. Original sound regenerates original program/bank events. Percussion and tracks sharing channels are excluded from independent changes rather than altering another track. Imported instrument settings are not mutated.
